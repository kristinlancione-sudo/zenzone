Our Programs

Classes for Every Age

Structured, age-appropriate sessions at Palmetto Bay Recreation Center

3:00 PM

Little Movers

Ages 4–6 • 55 minutes

Playful movement, simple breathing exercises, and calming routines designed for our youngest learners. We use storytelling, games, and imagination to teach self-regulation in a fun, engaging way.

  • Movement-based learning
  • Emotion recognition games
  • Simple breathing techniques
  • Rest and relaxation time
4:00 PM

Focus Club

Ages 7–10 • 55 minutes

Structured yoga flows, mindfulness practices, and tools for focus and emotional regulation. Kids learn techniques they can use during tests, homework, and moments of stress.

  • Yoga sequences for strength & flexibility
  • Mindfulness & meditation practice
  • Stress management tools
  • Group discussions & reflection
5:00 PM

Family Wind-Down

All ages + caregiver • 55 minutes

A gentle, restorative class for children and their caregivers to practice together. Wind down from the day with calming movement, partner poses, and breathing exercises the whole family can use at home.

  • Parent-child bonding activities
  • Partner yoga poses
  • Guided relaxation for all ages
  • Take-home calming practices
